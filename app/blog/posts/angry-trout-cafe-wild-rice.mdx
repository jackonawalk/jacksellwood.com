From a handwritten recipe card credited to the Angry Trout Café in Grand Marais, Minnesota.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups uncooked wild rice
- 2½ cups water
- ¼ cup chopped red onion
- ½ cup chopped celery
- 2 tablespoons canola oil, divided
- 4 ounces (about 1 cup) chopped fresh shiitake mushrooms
- ¼ teaspoon salt
- 1 tablespoon tamari (similar to soy sauce)
- ⅓ cup dried cranberries
- ¾ cup frozen peas
- Chopped hazelnuts, for garnish

## Directions

1. Combine the wild rice and water in a covered pan and bring to a boil over low heat. Simmer until the rice is tender but still has a bite, about 20–25 minutes for hand-harvested wild rice. Paddy-grown wild rice takes longer. Drain any excess water.
2. While the rice cooks, sauté the red onion and celery in 1 tablespoon of canola oil, then set them aside. Sauté the shiitake mushrooms in the remaining tablespoon of oil.
3. Toast the chopped hazelnuts in a dry pan over low heat until fragrant.
4. Stir the onion, celery, mushrooms, salt, tamari, dried cranberries, and frozen peas into the hot rice. Top with the toasted hazelnuts.
